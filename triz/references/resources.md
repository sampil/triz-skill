# Resources

A resource is anything already available in or near the system that can be used to solve the
problem. The best solutions use resources that are free, ready to use and already present.
List resources before generating ideas, then ask: "which of these could be X in the IFR?"

## Checklist

**Substance resources**: the materials and objects in the system, the product being processed,
waste and by-products, the environment (air, water, soil), cheap substances nearby. Modified
forms: the same substance in another state (melted, frozen, foamed, powdered, mixed).

**Field and energy resources**: mechanical (gravity, pressure, vibration, motion of parts),
thermal, chemical, electrical and magnetic fields, light, sound, energy that is currently wasted.

**Space resources**: empty space, cavities, the other side of a surface, unused dimensions,
gaps between parts, the space around the system.

**Time resources**: time before and after the operation, pauses and idle periods, parallel time
(doing two things at once), time when the user is waiting anyway.

**Information resources**: signals the system already emits, data already collected, existing
measurements, side channels (sound, heat, color change), user behavior that reveals intent.

**Functional resources**: capabilities components have beyond their current role; the ability of
a component to do its job and something else; standard interfaces that already exist.

**Human and social resources** (for product and business problems): the users themselves and
their motivation, the community, partners, existing habits and rituals, people already present
at the moment of need.

**Software and organizational resources**: existing services, caches, logs, idle compute, open
connections, schedules, existing contracts, brand trust, distribution channels.

## Priority order

1. Resources inside the operating zone during the operating time.
2. Resources in the system as a whole.
3. Resources in the environment and supersystem.
4. Derived resources (modified forms of existing ones).
5. New resources only as a last resort, ideally cheap, short-lived or self-removing.

## Example

Problem: support team overwhelmed by repetitive questions in a SaaS product.
Resources: experienced users (human), existing help articles and past tickets (information),
the moment of error in the UI (time and place), error messages already shown (field: the UI
itself), the community forum (substance in the supersystem).
IFR: "the moment of the error itself answers the question." Idea: context-aware help inside the
error message, generated from resolved tickets on the same error code; power users recognized
for answers in-product.
